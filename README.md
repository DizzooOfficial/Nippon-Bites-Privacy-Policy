# Nippon Bites: Food Tycoon — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 27 October 2026**

**Nippon Bites: Food Tycoon** is developed and published by **Dizzoo Studios** (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our game on Android or iOS.

## 1. Information We Handle

### a) Game Progress and Settings

The game stores information locally on your device to support gameplay, including:

- Restaurant progress, unlocked areas and upgrades.
- In-game cash, gems and reward progress.
- Offline earnings and daily reward availability.
- Sound, music, haptics and other preferences.

This information allows the game to restore your progress between sessions. The current version does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address or phone number.

### b) Advertising

We use **Google AdMob** to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads.

Depending on your device, advertising settings and applicable permissions, Google’s advertising services may collect or process information such as:

- IP address and approximate location derived from it.
- Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers.
- App interactions, including ad views and taps.
- Diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data.

This information may be used to deliver and measure advertisements, prevent fraud and maintain advertising services.

For more information, see [Google’s advertising privacy information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) and [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### c) Support Requests

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information you choose to provide. We use this information to respond to your request and resolve reported issues.

Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information described in this policy is used to:

- Save and restore game progress and preferences.
- Calculate offline earnings and manage in-game rewards.
- Display advertisements and verify rewarded-ad completion.
- Support game operation and respond to support requests.
- Help advertising providers measure performance and prevent abuse.

## 3. Notifications

The game may request permission to send local notifications, such as reminders to return to your restaurant and collect offline earnings.

These reminders are scheduled on your device. You can allow or disable notifications through your device’s settings.

## 4. In-Game Currency

The game includes virtual cash and gems earned through gameplay and rewarded advertisements. Gems can also be exchanged for virtual cash.

The current version does not process real-money purchases or collect payment-card information.

## 5. Third-Party Services and Links

The game uses Google AdMob, which processes information under Google’s privacy policies.

The game may also contain links to our other games, app-store listings, websites or social-media pages. Opening these links takes you to services operated under their own privacy policies.

We do not control how those external services handle your information.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not operate a server that receives your locally saved restaurant progress.

Advertising information may be collected and shared by Google and its advertising partners as described in their policies. Information you send to us for support may be processed through our email provider.

We may disclose information we hold where required by law or necessary to protect users, investigate abuse or protect our legal rights.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally saved progress remains on your device until it is removed through available device controls or the app is uninstalled. Copies may remain in device or operating-system backups, depending on your settings.

Support correspondence is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to address your request and meet applicable obligations.

Third-party providers determine retention periods for information they collect. Their policies explain available deletion and privacy controls.

## 8. Your Choices

Depending on your device and location, you may be able to:

- Manage notification permissions.
- Limit advertising personalization or manage advertising identifiers.
- Allow or deny tracking permissions where requested.
- Remove locally stored game data through device settings.
- Contact us about information you have provided directly.

Where processing requires consent, permission or another legal basis, this Privacy Policy does not replace that requirement. Simply installing or playing the game does not constitute consent to optional advertising personalization or tracking.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us to make a request concerning information we hold.

## 9. Children’s Privacy

We do not ask players to submit personal information as part of normal gameplay.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us. We will review the request and take appropriate action, including deletion where applicable.

## 10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information under our control. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game or our information-handling practices change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated date.

Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent for relevant changes.
